Lumen Relay

Tap mirrors to bend light into the crystals, without touching a bomb.

This one needs a desktop browser. It will not run properly on a phone, so rather than show you a broken canvas, here are the places it does work.

How to play

Tap a mirror to rotate it. Undo, hint and retry are limited.

About

A prototype. A beam leaves an emitter and you rotate mirrors to steer it into every matching receiver inside a move limit. Splitters fork it, tint gates recolour it, and bombs end the level. Every board is generated and then verified: it ships only if it is solvable, has few enough solutions, and a random bot wins it as rarely as that level intends.
