Perigee

Hold to orbit a planet, let go to sling. Endless gravity arcade.

This one needs a desktop browser. It will not run properly on a phone, so rather than show you a broken canvas, here are the places it does work.

How to play

Hold to orbit. Release to sling.

About

The landscape sibling to Jetpack Slinger. Hold to fall into orbit around the nearest planet, release to sling yourself onward. Bounce pads on the rails throw you back into play; everything else on them does not.
