Jetpack Slinger

Hold to hook a pole, release at the top of the swing, climb.

This one needs a desktop browser. It will not run properly on a phone, so rather than show you a broken canvas, here are the places it does work.

How to play

Hold anywhere to hook. Release to fly.

About

A one-thumb vertical climber. You get a single jetpack burn to leave the ground; after that every metre is stolen from a swing. Hold anywhere to throw a tether at the nearest pole, let go at the top of the arc, and you fire off in whatever direction you were already going. Release early and you waste it; release late and gravity has already taken it back.
